Important

Results vary from patient to patient.

Medical and cosmetic outcomes depend on diagnosis, skin type, treatment parameters, previous procedures and many individual factors. The page is designed to explain available services, not to promise a specific result.

Before booking

Bring context to your consultation.

  • Previous dermatology diagnoses or prescriptions
  • Past laser or aesthetic procedures
  • Current medications and relevant medical history
  • Photos showing progression, when useful
