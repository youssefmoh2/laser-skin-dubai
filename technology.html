The clinic lists a broad range of laser and energy-based technologies. The role of consultation is to match the technology to the diagnosis, skin type, treatment goal and individual clinical context.

Pigment · Tattoo · Rejuvenation

Pico Laser

The clinic lists Pico Laser for concerns including hyperpigmentation, melasma, selected tattoos, post-acne pigmentation and skin rejuvenation. Its role depends on the type and depth of pigment, previous treatments and skin characteristics.

MelasmaPigmentationTattoo RemovalPost-acne Concerns
Clinical ideaTechnology is selected after assessing pigment type, depth, skin tone and treatment history rather than by concern name alone.
Resurfacing · Texture · Scarring

CO₂ Fractional Laser

Dr. Mehta’s official CO₂ Fractional Laser page describes its use for post-acne scars, wrinkles, sun-damaged skin, stretch marks and other textural irregularities, while also discussing skin-tightening effects and collagen remodeling.

Post-acne ScarsTextureWrinklesSun DamageStretch Marks
How it worksFractional treatment creates controlled microscopic treatment zones while leaving surrounding skin untreated to support recovery.
Non-surgical tightening

TITAN

The clinic’s TITAN page describes an infrared-light technology intended for non-surgical skin tightening, including the face, neck and selected body areas. It is presented as a non-ablative option designed to heat the dermis while protecting the skin surface.

FaceNeckAbdomenArmsThighs
ExperienceDr. Mehta’s official site reports extensive clinical experience using TITAN across different skin types and body areas.
Texture · Pores · Scars
