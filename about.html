Dr. Mahaveer Mehta, MDDermatologist · Laser Specialist · Chairman & Medical Director
A regional career with global reach

From department leadership to pioneering laser dermatology.

Dr. Mehta has led his own medical center in Dubai since May 1990. Earlier, he served as Head of Dermatology at the Qatar Armed Forces Medical Unit and the International Hospital Bahrain, and held faculty roles at Al Fateh University in Tripoli and SMS Medical College in Jaipur.

His official profile describes him as a pioneer in introducing lasers to the Middle East, alongside decades of clinical practice, research, international conference activity and academic involvement.
