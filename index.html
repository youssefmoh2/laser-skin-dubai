The Doctor

Experience Shaped
Over Decades.

Dr. Mahaveer Mehta has more than 41 years of medical experience across the GCC and the UAE, and has led his own medical center in Dubai as Chairman and Medical Director since May 1990. He is internationally recognized as a pioneer in introducing laser dermatology to the Middle East.

Before establishing his Dubai practice, he served as Chairman of the Department of Dermatology at the Qatar Armed Forces Medical Unit and at the International Hospital Bahrain, and held faculty positions at Al Fateh University in Tripoli, Libya, and SMS Medical College in Jaipur, India. He has authored more than twenty research publications in international journals and continues to serve on the editorial boards of numerous dermatology journals worldwide.
